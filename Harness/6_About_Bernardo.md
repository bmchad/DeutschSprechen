## People & places that keep coming up

Recurring nouns worth owning properly — with article and plural.

| Who / what | German | Note |
|:--|:--|:--|
| electrical engineering | **die Elektrotechnik** (no pl.) | The field. The person is **der Ingenieur, die Ingenieure** — *Ich studiere Elektrotechnik* · later *Ich bin Elektroingenieur* |
| machine | **die Maschine, die Maschinen** | Plural already ends in `-n`, so the Dativ plural adds nothing: *mit Maschinen* |
| Germany | **Deutschland** (das, no article) | Singular: *Deutschland **ist*** |
| hydrogen | **der Wasserstoff** (no pl.) | "water-stuff". Spoken *H2* = "Ha-zwei". Hydrogen car: **das Wasserstoffauto** |
| electric car | **das Elektroauto, die Elektroautos** | Loanword `-s` plural — correct, and the exception to the `-s` Golden Rule |
| his university | **die Poli-USP** | Takes the article: *an **der** Poli-USP studieren*, *von **der** Poli-USP* |
| the car industry | **die Autoindustrie** · **die Elektroautoindustrie** | Compound takes the last part's gender: **die** Industrie |
