# Vocab & Nuance

Every word missed, looked up, or gotten wrong. **A word looked up is a word not known** — single-word lookups land here automatically with `Miss: 1`.

Maintained by the tutor. Feeds [[5_Review_Queue]] and the §8 example sentences.

**Nouns are always stored with article + plural.** `das Fenster, die Fenster` — never bare.

| Column | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| `Miss` | Times wrong or looked up |
| `Att` | Times the word was in play |
| `Streak` | Consecutive correct, unprompted, since the last miss |
| `St` | 🔴 new · 🟡 learning · 🟢 solid |

**Solid** = `Streak ≥ 3`, unprompted, across ≥ 2 sessions and ≥ 2 different contexts. Sort by `Miss ÷ Att` descending.
