# Vocab & Nuance

Every word missed, looked up, or gotten wrong. **A word looked up is a word not known** — single-word lookups land here automatically with `Miss: 1`.

Maintained by the tutor. Feeds [[5_Review_Queue]] and the §8 example sentences.

**Nouns are always stored with article + plural.** `das Fenster, die Fenster` — never bare.

| Column | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| `Miss` | Times wrong or looked up |
| `Att` | Times the word was in play |
| `Streak` | Consecutive correct, unprompted, since the last miss |
| `St` | 🔴 new · 🟡 learning · 🟢 solid |

**Solid** = `Streak ≥ 3`, unprompted, across ≥ 2 sessions and ≥ 2 different contexts. Sort by `Miss ÷ Att` descending.

## Nuance & Near-Synonyms

Two German words, one English word, and the choice matters.

| Concept | Options | When to use which | Miss | Att | St | Last |
|:--|:--|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:--|
| to know | `wissen` / `kennen` | `wissen` = facts · `kennen` = people & places | | | | |
| to eat | `essen` / `fressen` | `fressen` is for animals — using it for a person is an insult | | | | |
| probably / maybe | `wahrscheinlich` / `vermutlich` / `vielleicht` | Confidence ladder: **wahrscheinlich** ≈ 75% (probably) · **vermutlich** ≈ 60% (presumably, a guess) · **vielleicht** ≈ 40% (maybe, genuinely open) | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| **even** | `noch` / `sogar` / *(omit)* | **Three English uses, and only two need a German word.** ① `noch` + comparative = *even bigger* → **noch größer**. ② `sogar` = surprising inclusion → *Sogar der König kam.* ③ In a negative or `ohne` clause, **German just drops it**: *without even thinking* → **ohne darüber nachzudenken**. **Test:** can you say "still more"? → `noch`. "Including, surprisingly"? → `sogar`. Neither? → leave it out. ⚠️ `überhaupt` and `auch nur` also cover ③ but are **B1** — park them | 2 | 2 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| **actually** | `eigentlich` / `wirklich` / `tatsächlich` / `übrigens` | **Four jobs, one English word — and ⚠️ none of them is `aktuell`** (= current). **eigentlich** ✅A2 = in fact, contrary to expectation · **wirklich** = really, truly · **tatsächlich** = in reality, emphatic · **übrigens** = by the way. Hook: `eigen` (own) → "in its own right" | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| **to reach** | `suchen nach` / `meinen` / `greifen nach` / `erreichen` | **suchen nach** + **Dat** = search for (best for words) · **meinen** + Akk = to mean · **greifen nach** + **Dat** = physically reach for · **erreichen** + Akk = reach a place, goal or person. ⚠️ English "reach **for**" → German **`nach`**, never `für`, and `nach` takes Dativ | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| **for example** | `zum Beispiel` (z. B.) | **Never `für Beispiel`.** Fixed phrase, and a hidden Dativ: `zu` + `dem Beispiel` → **zum**. `zu` always takes Dativ, so every `z. B.` you write is a case you got right | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| **part / piece** | `der Teil` / `das Stück` | **der Teil, die Teile** = a conceptual division of a whole — *ein Teil unseres Lebens*, *der erste Teil*, *zum Teil* (partly). **das Stück, die Stücke** = a physical piece you could cut or hold — *ein Stück Kuchen*, *ein Stück Papier*; also a play (*Theaterstück*) and a counting unit (*5 Stück*). **Test:** could you pick it up? → `Stück`. Note the genders differ: **der** Teil vs **das** Stück | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
| the future | `in Zukunft` / `in der Zukunft` | **in Zukunft** = from now on, a change starting now · **in der Zukunft** = at some unspecified later point | 1 | 1 | 🔴 | 2026-09-21 |
